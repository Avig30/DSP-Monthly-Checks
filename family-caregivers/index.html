Frequently asked questions

Can a relative or trusted person be paid to help?

Yes. A parent, sibling, or other trusted person can become paid as their loved one's DSP (Direct Support Professional).

What happens first?

You call or send the short form. Your Family Program Coordinator listens, explains the program, and lets you know the next step.

Can ASG meet with our family at home?

Yes. If you prefer, someone from ASG can visit your home and walk you through the process in person.

What support continues after services begin?

Your Family Program Coordinator stays in touch and continues supporting your family for as long as services continue.
