Frequently asked questions

Can a relative or trusted person become the paid DSP?

Yes. The person is hired as a regular ASG W-2 DSP and completes the same required hiring, checks, training and timekeeping setup as other DSPs.

Does my family member need DDD approval already?

You can call ASG at any stage. Once the DDD application is complete, DSP onboarding can begin while approval is pending. Paid services begin only after authorization is active.

Who sets the weekly schedule?

Your family chooses the schedule within the approved service hours.

Will ASG contact my support coordinator?

Only with your permission. Your Family Program Coordinator contacts the coordinator to confirm the service, the SDR hours and the remaining steps.

How do I track time?

You can use the HHAeXchange app or a paper timesheet. Your Family Program Coordinator helps set up the option you choose and practices it with you before the first shift.

How much is the pay?

The final hourly rate depends on the approved service, hours and available budget. ASG confirms the rate with you before the written offer.

Can someone meet us at home?

Yes. An ASG team member can come to your family's home and walk you through the process.
