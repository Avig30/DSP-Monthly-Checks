Two ways to get paid

Self-direction is DDD's alternative to traditional provider-managed services. Both paths are legitimate - here's the honest difference.

Self-directed
With ASG (agency)
Who's the employer
With Acumen, the individual or a chosen representative is the employer of record. With Easterseals NJ, Easterseals is the employer of record and the individual is the managing employer.
ASG - we hire you as a Direct Support Professional.
Administration, payroll, taxes
The fiscal intermediary manages payroll and taxes. The individual or managing employer still chooses, hires, and directs workers and approves timesheets.
ASG handles agency administration, payroll, and taxes.
How your pay is set
Your family sets a "reasonable and customary" wage the budget can support.
Pay starts at $25 an hour. Your exact rate depends on the approved service, your approved hours, and the available budget - confirmed in writing before you start.
Backup when you need a break
Your family arranges it - including securing coverage when staff call out.
ASG may help arrange a trained DSP when coverage is available. Arranged when possible, never promised.

Self-direction fits families who want to be the employer and manage the details. If you'd rather not carry the employer load, that's what we're for.

Questions families ask

Plain answers to what families ask us most before they call.

Can a family member really get paid to provide care in New Jersey?

Yes. Through NJ DDD, a family member can be hired as a paid Direct Support Professional - either through an agency like ASG or through self-direction. Your support coordinator can confirm which path fits.

How is my pay set - is it really $25 an hour?

Pay starts at $25 an hour. Your exact rate depends on the approved service, your approved hours, and the available budget - confirmed in writing before you start. We quote you a starting rate, not a best-case number.

Who qualifies?

An adult New Jersey resident with a documented intellectual or developmental disability, enrolled with NJ DDD or likely eligible. Not sure about DDD status? We'll explain how DDD eligibility works and where support coordination fits.

Which family members can be considered?

Parents, siblings, relatives, or trusted people may be considered, depending on the approved service and our normal hiring requirements. Talk to us and your support coordinator for a straight answer.

Do I need experience or a certification?

No. If you've been caring for your loved one, you already have the hard part. We provide the training, coaching, and ongoing support.

Will getting paid affect my loved one's benefits?

Many families ask this. We'll walk through it with you and your support coordinator so you understand how it works before anything is finalized. You won't navigate it alone.

What's the difference between going through ASG and self-directing?

With ASG, you're hired as our Direct Support Professional and we carry the administration, payroll, and taxes. In self-direction, your family acts as the employer, with payroll processed through a fiscal intermediary. See "Two ways to get paid" above for the full comparison.

What happens after I call or send the form?

A Family Program Coordinator talks with you about your situation. With your permission, we coordinate the program directly with your support coordinator and guide you through onboarding, training, and hiring, step by step.

What if I need a break?

ASG may help arrange a trained DSP when coverage is available. It's arranged when possible, never promised - we'll always be honest about what's possible.

What if my loved one isn't confirmed with DDD yet?

That's okay - many families start here. We'll explain how DDD eligibility works and where support coordination fits.
