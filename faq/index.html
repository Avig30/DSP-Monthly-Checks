Getting paid to care for family

Can I really get paid to care for my own family member?
If your family member qualifies for New Jersey's DDD (Division of Developmental Disabilities) program, a parent, sibling, or other relative can become their paid DSP. We handle the enrollment, training, scheduling, and payroll.
Who in my family can be paid?
Parents, siblings, and other relatives can step into the DSP role. Every family's situation is different - reach out and we will confirm exactly what works for yours.
My loved one is not enrolled with DDD yet. Can we still start?
Talk to us anyway. We will tell you exactly what the next step is and walk you through it.
What training is required?
DSPs complete the required training, with our team walking you through every step.
What if I need a break?
That is what our In-Home Respite service is for - a trained, pre-screened DSP you trust steps in while you rest.

Finding a DSP & our services

What is a DSP?
A Direct Support Professional is a trained professional who works one-on-one with your loved one - at home and out in the community.
How do you match a DSP with my loved one?
We pre-screen every DSP, match on personality and schedule, and you meet your match before anything starts. Then we stay involved.
What services does ASG provide?
Individual Supports, Community-Based Supports, Behavioral Supports, and In-Home Respite.
Which areas do you serve?
All 21 New Jersey counties.
How fast can services start?
Every family's timeline is different - it depends on DDD enrollment and finding the right match. Call us and we will walk you through what to expect for your situation.

Becoming a DSP

Do I need experience to become a DSP?
No. Whether you have care experience or are brand new, we will train you, with ongoing coaching included.
How do I apply?
The application on our Become a DSP page takes about two minutes. We take it from there.
What support do DSPs get at ASG?
Matches that fit your schedule and your strengths, support from a team that answers the phone, and competitive pay, always on time.
