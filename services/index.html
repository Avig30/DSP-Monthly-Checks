SERVICES

DDD services for your loved one, across all of New Jersey.

ASG Home Care is approved by DDD - New Jersey's Division of Developmental Disabilities. We support people with intellectual and developmental disabilities at home and in the community, with care from a trained DSP or from a family member we hire and train. Every plan is built around your loved one's goals.

Support built around real life

Every person deserves to live with dignity, purpose, and confidence. Our services support the individual and the family around them. We meet people where they are - at home, in their neighborhoods, in their daily routines - and we work on practical skills, stronger relationships, and structure that lasts. And when a family member steps into the DSP role, we train and support them for as long as they need it.

Individual Supports

One-on-one care focused on life skills, communication, and independence - at home or out in the community. Cooking, money management, daily routines: we build the plan around your loved one's goals.

Community-Based Supports (CBS)

Help getting out and staying active: transportation, errands, social outings, recreation. Our DSPs help individuals build confidence and stay connected to their community.

Behavioral Supports

Personalized behavior plans, created and overseen by trained specialists, to reduce unsafe behaviors and build positive routines at home and in the community.

In-Home Respite

Short-term care that gives families a real break, while your loved one stays safe and comfortable at home. Scheduled regularly or as needed, always with a DSP you trust.

Why guidance for everyday life matters

Independence is built in morning routines, grocery trips, and ordinary afternoons. Consistent one-on-one support turns those moments into progress, and gives families peace of mind that someone reliable is by their loved one's side.

What to expect: 4 simple steps

1

Reach Out

Fill out our Find a DSP form or call our office. From the first call, our team listens and explains what to expect, so you always know the next step.

2

Review Your Needs

We take time to understand your family's situation: what supports are needed, when, and how you want them delivered. A truly personalized plan, built around your family.

3

Choose Your Path

You decide: have a family member step into the DSP role with our full training and guidance, or let us match you with one of our pre-screened professionals. Either way, the right fit for your loved one.

4

Begin Services

Once everything is in place, services can begin quickly. We stay with you: regular check-ins, support for your DSP, and real attention to progress.

Still have questions? We are here to help.

Whether you are a family exploring DDD services for the first time, a support coordinator looking for a reliable partner, or someone interested in becoming a DSP - our team is ready to guide you. (A support coordinator is the state-assigned case manager who helps your family plan services. We work with them every day.)
